What it is

The Gu Yue Clan is Fang Yuan's clan, and the only society he belongs to in his second life. Its world is small and its hierarchy is total: elders above, youths ranked by Aptitude, and mortals without it at the very bottom.

What its elders say about it

The clearest account of the clan's health comes from its own people during the Aperture Opening Ceremony, and it is not flattering.

Alas, our bloodline grows ever more diluted, and few Rank 4 experts have emerged in recent years to strengthen it.

Where it came from

Not here. The clan is a transplant.

Who runs it

A Gu Yue Clan Leader and a bench of elders, including Gu Yue Chi Lian and Gu Yue Mo Chen, whose rivalry plays out in public over children's exam results.

Why it matters

The clan is the first thing Fang Yuan has to work within, and he has already watched it live and die once. Everything about it, from the ritual and elders to the ceremony itself, is a machine for deciding which children are worth feeding, and he intends to be one of them while he builds something far larger.
